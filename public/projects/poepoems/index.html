A poem in two voices

Write with Poe

Offer a word. Let his language answer.

You write one word, then Poe contributes the next. This program counts which words followed yours in a collection of his writing, then picks one at random. Words he used more often in that position are more likely to appear.

It remembers word pairs, not the meaning of your poem. Treat it as a creative exercise: follow the surprises, shape the lines, and see what emerges. If your word has no recorded next word, you get another turn.

Your shared poem

You Poe

Every haunting begins with a word.

Loading Poe’s words…

One word at a time, without punctuation. Apostrophes within words are welcome. Review, then confirm.

Two voices. One poem.

A collaboration across time Built from word pairs in Poe’s collected writing.
